Race Audio — test page

The overlay below is the real Twitch extension, running on a fake stream. Tap a runner to hear only them.

runner 1 runner 2

What to expect

  1. Tap runner 1: a steady low tone (440 Hz), nothing else.
  2. Tap runner 2: a higher tone (880 Hz). The first one stops.
  3. Tap the headphones button for the mixer: commentary is a lower tone again (220 Hz), and each slider should change only its own runner.
  4. Silence with the chips lit means the feeds have expired — they are test tones, not a live race.

Why this page exists

  1. On your phone with wifi off, this proves audio reaches a viewer from outside the house — the part a test from indoors cannot show.
  2. Relay: race.luncearnnburn.com. Reading feeds is open; publishing needs a password.
