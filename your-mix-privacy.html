Your Mix — Privacy Notice

Last updated 4 October 2026

Your Mix is a Twitch Extension that lets you choose which audio from a stream you hear, and at what volume — the streamer's voice, the game, the alerts, or each runner in a race — and balance them yourself.

It is deliberately simple about data: listening requires no account, no sign-in and no identifier of any kind.

What is sent when you listen

When you turn a slider above zero, your browser opens a direct audio connection to the relay that carries the stream's audio feeds (mix.luncearnnburn.com). That request contains only the technical details needed to set up the connection. No Twitch username, user ID or token is sent, and the relay has no way to tell one listener from another.

As with any server you connect to, the relay necessarily receives your IP address in order to send audio back to you.

What is sent if you are a broadcaster or moderator

If you are the broadcaster of the channel, or one of its moderators, and you open the control panel, the extension sends the signed token that Twitch issues to the extension so the relay can confirm you are allowed to make changes. That token is issued by Twitch and identifies you only by an opaque ID, not by name or email. It is used to check permission for that action and nothing else.

What is stored on your device

One setting: your audio sync offset, remembered separately for each channel so you do not have to set it again. It is kept in your browser's local storage, it never leaves your device, and clearing your browser data removes it.

What this extension does not do

  • No analytics, tracking pixels, fingerprinting or advertising.
  • No cookies.
  • No microphone, camera or location access.
  • No personal information is collected, sold, rented or shared.
  • No profiles are built about you, and nothing is linked to your Twitch identity.

Logs

The relay keeps ordinary server logs of connections, which include IP addresses, for a short time so that faults can be diagnosed. They are not used to identify or profile anyone, and are not shared with third parties.

Twitch

The extension runs inside Twitch and loads Twitch's own extension helper script. Anything Twitch itself collects is covered by Twitch's privacy notice, not this one.

Children

Your Mix is not directed at children and collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this notice changes, the date at the top changes with it.
